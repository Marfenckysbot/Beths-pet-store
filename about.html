WHY WE'RE HERE

A little more joy in every pet's day.

Beth's Pet Store is being built as a welcoming marketplace connecting pet lovers with independent businesses, useful essentials and thoughtful care resources. We want great small pet businesses to be easier to discover — and everyday pet care to feel a little easier.

OUR PURPOSE

Better discovery

Give independent pet shops, makers and specialist suppliers a place to introduce their work and connect with people who care about animals.

OUR PROMISE

Honesty over hype

We won't invent customer reviews, seller credentials, stock numbers or live discounts. Preview products are clearly marked until real sellers and inventory are ready.

OUR DIRECTION

For pet lovers everywhere

Built with an international outlook for independent pet businesses and customers. Seller availability, shipping destinations and local requirements will be shown clearly as each market becomes operational.

OUR STANDARD

Pets come first

Animal welfare, responsible information and transparent seller review belong at the centre.
