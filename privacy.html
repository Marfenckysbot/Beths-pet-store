YOUR INFORMATION MATTERS

Privacy notice.

Last updated: November 2026. This notice describes the information collected through the seller interest form while Beth's Pet Store is being prepared as a multi-seller marketplace.

Information you submit

The seller interest form asks for a business or brand name, contact name, business email, country, an optional business website or social link, business category, a short description and your consent to be contacted about the application.

How we use it

We use these details to review seller applications, assess whether a business may be suitable for the marketplace and contact the applicant about next steps. Submitting the form does not create an account, approve a seller or publish a storefront.

Form processing

The seller interest form is configured for processing by Netlify, the hosting and form-processing provider used for this website. Information submitted through that form is handled by the provider as part of delivering the service and may be subject to its own privacy terms.

Information not to submit

Do not submit passwords, payment-card details, bank credentials, government identity documents or other highly sensitive information through the seller interest form. If further checks are needed, a secure process must be arranged separately.

Accounts, payments and tracking

The current website does not provide customer accounts, seller dashboards, online checkout, seller payouts or live inventory. The new storefront pages do not use an analytics tracking ID. These capabilities may be introduced later with updated notices and appropriate service providers.

Questions and requests

If you have a privacy question about an application, reply to the application-related correspondence you receive from Beth's Pet Store. Please do not send sensitive identity or financial information by email.
