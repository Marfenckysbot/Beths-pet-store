YOUR INFORMATION MATTERS

Privacy notice.

Last updated: November 2026. This notice describes the current preview website and its seller, contact, support and feedback forms. It must be updated before customer accounts, payment processing or live marketplace services launch.

INFORMATION YOU SUBMIT

Forms

Forms may ask for your name, business name, email, country, business link, category and message. Only provide information needed for the enquiry. Do not submit passwords, card details, bank credentials or identity documents.

HOW IT IS USED

Responding to you

We use submitted details to review seller interest, respond to enquiries, provide support and consider feedback. A seller application is not approval and does not create an account or storefront.

FORM PROCESSING

Netlify

Forms are marked for Netlify Forms processing. Information submitted is handled by the hosting/form provider as part of delivering that service and may be subject to its privacy terms. Form capture and email notifications must be verified in the Netlify dashboard after deployment.

LOCAL PREVIEW FEATURES

Browser storage

The demo shopping bag and wishlist use local browser storage on the device. They do not create accounts, transmit an order or take payment. You can clear this data through your browser's site-data settings.

NOT CURRENTLY ACTIVE

Accounts and tracking

The preview does not provide customer accounts, seller dashboards, live inventory, online checkout or seller payouts. The new storefront does not use an analytics tracking ID.

YOUR QUESTIONS

Privacy requests

For a privacy enquiry, use the contact form and select the relevant topic. Do not include sensitive identity or financial information in the message.

This is a starter privacy notice, not legal advice. It must be reviewed and expanded for the actual data flows, service providers and laws applicable to the operating marketplace before launch.
