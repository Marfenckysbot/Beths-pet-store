YOUR INFORMATION MATTERS

Privacy notice.

Last updated: 12 November 2026. This notice describes the current pre-launch website, customer accounts and seller application workflow. It must be reviewed and expanded before live marketplace trading begins.

01 / INFORMATION

What we collect

If you create an account, Supabase Auth processes your email address and authentication credentials. Your account profile may include a display name and country. If you apply to become a seller, we process the business and application details you submit through the authenticated Seller Centre. Sellers who are approved may upload product information and product images.

02 / PURPOSE

How we use it

Account information is used to sign you in and provide account features. Seller application details are used for human review, approval and marketplace administration. Profile, wishlist and seller/product data support the features shown on the site. We do not currently create customer orders or payment transactions.

03 / SERVICE PROVIDERS

Hosting and storage

The website is hosted by Vercel. Account authentication, database records and product image storage are provided by Supabase. The connected Supabase project is currently hosted in the US East region. The site also loads web fonts from Google Fonts and an illustrative 3D model from a third-party asset host. These providers may process technical information needed to deliver their services; review their privacy notices before using the site.

04 / BROWSER STORAGE

Bag and wishlist

The preview shopping bag is stored in your browser and does not place an order. Guest wishlist preferences may be stored locally; signed-in account wishlist data is stored with the marketplace account. Supabase authentication session data is managed by the Supabase client in the browser. You can clear local site data through your browser settings, but doing so may sign you out or clear local selections.

05 / FORMS AND PAYMENTS

What is not active

The contact, support and feedback forms are temporarily disabled while a secure Vercel-compatible submission service is prepared. Seller applications are submitted through the authenticated Seller Centre. Checkout, payment collection, refunds, seller payouts and delivery booking are disabled. Do not enter card details, bank credentials, passwords or identity documents into any general enquiry channel.

06 / SHARING AND SECURITY

Access controls

We do not sell personal information. Supabase row-level security restricts access to account and marketplace records according to the user's role. Administrator access is limited to the provisioned marketplace administrator. No security measure can guarantee absolute protection; keep your password private and sign out on shared devices.

07 / RETENTION

Keeping information

Account and seller application information is retained in the connected Supabase project while the pre-launch service is being developed. A formal retention and deletion schedule, operational privacy request channel and jurisdiction-specific data transfer review must be completed before commercial launch.

08 / YOUR CHOICES

Privacy requests

A verified public privacy contact channel has not yet been configured. The contact form is currently unavailable, so do not submit sensitive information through the website. This notice will be updated with a working privacy request channel before launch.

This is a factual starter notice, not legal advice or a complete privacy policy. It must be reviewed for the laws and countries in which the marketplace will operate, including international data transfers, user rights, retention, cookies and the final service-provider list.
