THE QUICK ANSWERS

Good questions. Clear answers.

MARKETPLACE

Can I buy products today?

The catalogue is a visual preview. The bag is a local demo only; live checkout and payments are not enabled.

SELLERS

Can I list my business?

You can submit a seller interest application. It is reviewed by a person and does not automatically create an account or publish products.

PRICES

Are the sale prices real?

No. Crossed-out prices and discounts in the preview catalogue are illustrative, not live promotions.

DELIVERY

Do you deliver worldwide?

International reach is a design goal, not a live shipping service. Delivery details depend on confirmed providers.

ADOPTION

Are pets available?

There are no live verified adoption listings yet. We plan to work with real welfare partners.

PAYMENTS

Can sellers receive payouts?

Not yet. Payment processing and payouts need approved merchant accounts and a secure backend.
