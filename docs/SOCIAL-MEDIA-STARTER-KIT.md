# Beth's Pet Store — social media starter kit

## Brand positioning

**A growing marketplace for pets, pet essentials and animal care.**

Voice: warm, useful, trustworthy, inclusive and playful. Avoid medical claims, exaggerated promises and claims that Beth already has a large seller network.

Suggested handle to check: @BethsPetStore. Availability is not verified. Use the same handle and logo across Instagram, TikTok, Facebook and WhatsApp Business where available.

## First 10 organic content ideas

1. Meet Beth's Pet Store — explain the marketplace vision honestly.
2. Supplier call — invite established pet businesses to contact us.
3. Pet-owner checklist — what to prepare before bringing home a pet.
4. Equipment spotlight — explain how to choose a suitable carrier or cage without claiming a specific product is in stock.
5. Responsible pet ownership — long-term costs, time and welfare.
6. Behind the build — show website progress and explain what is still being prepared.
7. Ask the community — what pet supplies are hardest to find locally?
8. Safe shopping tips — check seller identity, product condition and receipts.
9. Vet visit reminder — encourage professional veterinary advice rather than self-medicating.
10. Supplier feature — only after the supplier has agreed and details are verified.

## Posting rhythm with no advertising budget

Start with three posts per week and a few stories when there is something useful to share. Repurpose the same original content across platforms, adapting the format. Reply to genuine comments and messages. Do not buy followers, scrape contact lists or send unsolicited bulk messages.

## Paid advertising readiness gate

Do not spend on Google Ads, TikTok Ads or Meta Ads until:
- The advertised products are real, available and permitted to advertise.
- The landing page and contact/order process work.
- Fulfilment, shipping, returns and support are tested.
- Business/payment setup is accepted by the provider and platform.
- A small test budget can be lost without affecting essential expenses.

Check current country, billing and category rules inside each ad platform before creating campaigns. Pharmaceutical/veterinary medicine and live-animal advertising can have additional restrictions. Never try to bypass platform review or misclassify products.

## Account ownership and security

The founder must create and verify accounts using an email address and phone number they control, enable two-factor authentication, store recovery codes privately and retain admin access. Never send passwords, one-time codes or recovery codes in chat. The assistant can prepare bios, captions, content calendars and creative briefs, but cannot complete platform identity/phone verification on the founder's behalf.
