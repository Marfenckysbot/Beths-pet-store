# Supplier outreach playbook

## Partnership proposition

Beth's Pet Store is preparing a curated online marketplace. The initial offer is to help established pet businesses reach more customers while the supplier retains control of its inventory and confirms each order. We are seeking a small number of pilot suppliers, not claiming to have an established customer base or guaranteed sales.

## First-contact message

Hello,

I'm reaching out on behalf of Beth's Pet Store, a pet marketplace currently preparing its supplier network.

We are looking to work with established pet businesses that can provide current product information and reliable fulfilment. Our proposed pilot is simple: with your permission, we feature selected products, confirm availability and price with you before accepting an order, and coordinate the customer handover or delivery under agreed terms.

Could you share:
- Your current product catalogue and trade/reseller pricing
- Which areas you can deliver to or support for collection
- How often stock and prices can be updated
- Your order confirmation and dispatch process
- Your returns/damaged-item policy
- Whether you offer wholesale, commission or supplier-direct fulfilment
- Any business or regulatory documents relevant to the products you supply

We are starting small and would like to agree on a transparent pilot before publishing any listings. Would you be open to a conversation?

Thank you,
Beth's Pet Store

## Supplier qualification checklist

Record only information needed for business verification and fulfilment. Keep identity documents in the supplier's secure channel; do not store sensitive documents in a public repository.

- [ ] Business name and public business address/operating area verified
- [ ] Named business contact and official phone/email verified
- [ ] Product catalogue received with date/version
- [ ] Permission to use product images, logos and descriptions
- [ ] Price, currency, taxes and price validity period confirmed
- [ ] Stock update frequency and inventory owner agreed
- [ ] Product condition, batch/expiry where relevant, and warranty confirmed
- [ ] Delivery coverage, fees, dispatch time and tracking method agreed
- [ ] Returns, cancellations, refunds, damaged/lost parcels and disputes agreed
- [ ] Invoice/receipt and who collects customer payment agreed
- [ ] Commission/wholesale margin and settlement timing agreed in writing
- [ ] Required licences/registrations verified for regulated categories
- [ ] Pilot order successfully completed before broader promotion

## Listing record template

| Field | Required information |
|---|---|
| Listing ID | Internal unique identifier |
| Supplier | Verified supplier business name |
| Product | Exact brand, model, size, variant |
| Category | Accessories, food, equipment, etc. |
| Price | Supplier-confirmed price and currency |
| Availability | In stock / request to order / unavailable |
| Last confirmed | Date and time supplier confirmed |
| Fulfilment | Supplier dispatch / agreed collection / delivery partner |
| Delivery area | Confirmed service area, not assumptions |
| Media permission | Yes/no and evidence reference |
| Compliance status | Not regulated / pending review / cleared |
| Listing status | Draft / approved / paused / removed |

## Initial pilot rules

- Do not scrape or republish another store's catalogue without permission.
- Do not invent stock, prices, ratings, reviews, warranties, delivery windows or seller credentials.
- Reconfirm stock and total price before taking payment.
- Do not accept payment into a personal account unrelated to the approved business arrangement.
- Do not publish veterinary drugs, vaccines, prescription medicines or animal listings until the applicable checks are completed.
- Remove or pause stale listings when the supplier cannot confirm them.
