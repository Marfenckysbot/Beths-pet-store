## Mandatory verification before any listing

1. Verify the legal business identity and the person authorised to agree terms.
2. Obtain written permission to publish product images, descriptions and prices.
3. For every food/feed/treatment/drug/vaccine item, verify the applicable NAFDAC registration or authorisation, product identity, batch/expiry, storage and distribution requirements. NAFDAC identifies veterinary drugs, vaccines, biologics, animal feeds and pet foods among regulated categories: https://nafdac.gov.ng/about-nafdac/nafdac-organisation/directorates/veterinary-medicine-and-allied-products/
4. Ask NAFDAC or qualified Nigerian counsel to confirm what permissions apply to Beth's role as a marketplace/intermediary, not only the supplier's role.
5. Agree written stock confirmation, pricing validity, payment collection, delivery, returns, refunds, complaints, product recalls and commission.
6. Do not accept money or promise availability until the supplier and fulfilment route are confirmed.
